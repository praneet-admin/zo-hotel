      Plans change. Your booking can too.

      Fewer guests or new dates? Here is how it works.

      • New dates? You pay the rate for the new nights, plus 20% on peak dates such as Diwali and New Year
      • Fewer guests? We adjust the rooms and show the new total
      • New dates fully booked? Your payment becomes 12-month Zo credit
      • Nothing changes until you confirm
      See all peak dates

      Demand is highest on these dates, so moving a booking into them adds 20% to the rate for those nights.

        Someone in our group can’t come. Is the booking lost?

        No. We adjust the rooms to fit your group and show the new total before anything changes.

        My Mumbai meeting moved. Can my stay move too?

        Yes. Choose a new night and pay that night’s rate. Peak dates, such as Diwali or New Year, add 20% because demand is highest then. If the night is fully booked, your payment becomes Zo credit for 12 months.

        My flight is late. Do I lose the room?

        No. The room is yours for the night you booked, and there’s no extra charge for arriving late.

        Are there rules for groups?

        No curfew. Visitors who aren’t staying sign in at the front desk, as the law requires.

        Can anyone change my booking without me?

        No. Every change shows the new price first, and nothing happens until you approve it.
