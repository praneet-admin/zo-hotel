      A friend dropped out. Is the Goa booking lost?

      No. Their room goes back, the other rooms keep their rate, and the new total shows on the booking.

      My Mumbai meeting moved. Can my stay move too?

      Yes. Pick the new night and pay that night’s rate. If it’s full, your money becomes Zo credit for 12 months.

      My flight is late. Do I lose the room?

      No. The room is yours for the night you booked, and there’s no extra charge for arriving late.

      We’re a big group. Are there strict rules?

      No curfew. Just one rule: visitors who aren’t staying sign in at the desk, as the law requires.

      Can anyone change my booking without me?

      No. Every change shows the new price first, and nothing happens until you approve it.
